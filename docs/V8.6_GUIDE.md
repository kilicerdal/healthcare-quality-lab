## New model-authored fields

Each line must include coverage_decision (covered/rejected), individual_deductible_remaining_cents and family_deductible_remaining_cents before choosing nominal deductible. Those values are authored by the model, not filled by the server. Policy inputs explicitly use names such as individual_deductible_limit_cents; financial outputs such as individual_deductible mean cumulative used amounts.

For covered lines the model must subtract prior usage from both deductible limits, floor remaining balances at zero and choose the minimum of eligible money and both remaining balances. Rejected lines keep prior account usage, report actual remaining balances and have zero cost-sharing worksheet amounts. Earlier covered lines affect later rejected-line balances.

The independent checker validates all three new fields after generation as well as every existing monetary, account, lifecycle and ledger expectation. A missing field, wrong decision or wrong remainder prevents export. No calculator answer or evaluator mismatch is passed into the next generation stage.

## Sealed development gate and fresh assessment

```sh
.venv/bin/python -m tools.evaluate_first_attempts_v86 --prepare artifacts/v8.6/my-plan.json
.venv/bin/python -m tools.evaluate_first_attempts_v86 --plan artifacts/v8.6/my-plan.json --state .local/my-study.sqlite --output-dir artifacts/v8.6/my-study
```

The plan seals source/cohort hashes and input-only tasks. Both exposed V8.5 failures are attempted before the fresh cases. A complete wrong exposed answer allows the other exposed case to be diagnosed; any exposed failure blocks fresh generation. Timeout/provider error stops further requests, because backend cancellation is best effort.

One candidate permits at most eight calls, 180 admitted seconds per case and a 600-second admission window. A full case budget plus preflight headroom must remain before another case begins. All skipped cases remain in the plan/results. No hidden retry, resume, model swap or budget extension occurs.

This version's development policy permits at most one generic revision after exposed-case failure and before any fresh inference, for twelve actual calls across candidates at most. Candidate paths/source identities remain separate. Fresh cases are never used to tune the candidate being assessed. Published H86 inputs are exposed now and must not be called fresh in later reruns.
